# DueSoon — Your Entire Academic Life, One Workspace

**An AI-powered academic productivity and study management platform built for university students.**

DueSoon helps students organize their academic life without constantly switching between different applications, websites, folders, and AI tools. From managing subject-wise lectures and study materials to preparing for exams, tracking assignments, planning study hours, and discovering career opportunities, DueSoon brings everything together in one centralized platform.

**One platform. Every subject. Smarter studying. Better opportunities.**

---

## 1. The Problem

University students deal with multiple academic responsibilities every day, but the tools they use to manage them are scattered across different platforms.

Some of the major challenges students face include:

### 1.1 Disorganized Lectures and Study Materials

Students attend multiple courses, each containing numerous lectures, presentations, PDFs, assignments, and other learning resources.

- Downloaded lectures become difficult to organize.
- Finding a particular lecture among hundreds of downloaded files is time-consuming.
- Maintaining the correct lecture sequence becomes difficult.
- Study materials from different subjects get mixed together.
- Students waste valuable study time searching for resources instead of learning.

### 1.2 Distractions While Learning

Students frequently use platforms such as YouTube to access educational lectures. However, recommended videos, unrelated content, and other distractions can interrupt their study sessions and reduce concentration.

### 1.3 Difficulties During Exam Preparation

When examinations approach, students often switch between multiple applications and browser tabs.

For example, they might:
- Upload lecture slides to ChatGPT to understand difficult concepts.
- Open another chat to summarize a PDF.
- Search websites for important examination topics.
- Use separate tools to generate practice questions.
- Manually organize the resulting notes.

This fragmented process makes exam preparation unnecessarily complicated and time-consuming.

### 1.4 Poor Academic Task Management

Students often have assignments, quizzes, examinations, projects, and daily study goals across several subjects.

Managing different deadlines and estimating how much time each task requires can become overwhelming.

### 1.5 Limited Awareness of Career Opportunities

Students also struggle to discover relevant internships, research programs, fellowships, and other career opportunities.

Opportunities are published across numerous websites, organizations, and platforms. Students may miss valuable opportunities simply because they cannot continuously monitor every source.

**The central problem:** Students do not lack educational resources. They lack one organized environment that connects their learning materials, academic responsibilities, AI-powered learning assistance, and career development.

---

## 2. Our Solution — DueSoon

DueSoon is a centralized, AI-powered academic productivity platform that allows students to manage their entire academic journey in one place.

Instead of switching between multiple applications, students can create dedicated workspaces for individual subjects and manage everything related to those subjects within their respective workspaces.

DueSoon combines:

- Subject-wise academic organization.
- Lecture management and playlists.
- Document and study material management.
- Built-in AI study assistance.
- Personal notes and learning resources.
- Subject-wise and centralized task management.
- Workload-aware study scheduling.
- Academic progress and grade tracking.
- Internship, fellowship, and research opportunity discovery.

The goal is to reduce the time students spend organizing their academic work and give them more time to actually study.

## 3. Core Features

### 3.1 Personalized Academic Dashboard

The dashboard provides students with a centralized overview of their academic progress and upcoming responsibilities.

**Key features:**
- Overview of enrolled subjects.
- Total pending academic tasks.
- Completed tasks.
- Today's planned study hours.
- Upcoming assignment and examination deadlines.
- Quick access to individual subject workspaces.
- Featured internships, fellowships, and research opportunities.
- Direct access to the study planner.

Students can understand their academic workload without opening every subject individually.

### 3.2 Dedicated Subject Workspaces

Every subject has its own independent workspace.

For example:
- Artificial Intelligence
- Data Structures and Algorithms
- Computer Fundamentals
- Information Security

Within each subject, students can organize their lectures, documents, notes, tasks, and AI conversations.

**Benefits:**
- Keeps resources from different subjects separate.
- Makes finding specific learning materials easier.
- Provides a structured environment for every course.
- Maintains subject-specific AI conversation history.

### 3.3 Smart Lecture Management

Students can organize their recorded lectures into structured playlists instead of keeping hundreds of video files in unorganized folders.

**Key features:**
- Upload multiple video lectures from a device.
- Add educational YouTube videos through their links.
- Arrange lectures in the correct numerical sequence.
- Reorder lectures according to learning requirements.
- Rename or delete lectures.
- Choose between list and grid views.
- Resume videos from the last watched timestamp.
- Adjust playback speed.
- Navigate between previous and next lectures.
- Automatically track watched lectures and progress.

This helps students follow their courses systematically without repeatedly searching for individual lectures.

### 3.4 Organized Study Materials and Built-in Notepad

Students can store and organize their academic resources inside the relevant subject workspace.

**Supported document formats include:**
- PDF
- PowerPoint (PPTX)
- Word (DOCX)
- Text files (TXT/MD)
- Code files

**Key features:**
- Create custom folders for lectures, notes, readings, and past examinations.
- Upload multiple study documents.
- Organize resources using drag-and-drop.
- Preview supported documents.
- Download uploaded files.
- Create multiple notes for each subject.
- Automatically save notes.
- Edit, download, and delete notes.

All learning resources remain organized according to their respective subjects.

### 3.5 AI-Powered Study Assistant

One of DueSoon's main features is its integrated AI Study Assistant.

Students no longer need to repeatedly switch between different AI applications and separate conversations for every subject.

Each subject has its own AI conversation history, helping students keep discussions relevant to their course materials.

**Key capabilities:**

- Ask questions about difficult topics.
- Upload relevant study documents.
- Get explanations based on course materials.
- Understand complex concepts in simpler language.
- Summarize lecture slides and documents.
- Generate examination practice questions.
- Receive step-by-step guidance for practice problems.
- Get assignment guidance and structure.

**Quick Actions:**

| Quick Action | Purpose |
|---|---|
| 💡 Explain Concepts | Understand difficult topics through step-by-step explanations. |
| 📋 Summarize Key Points | Extract important information from study materials. |
| 🎯 Quiz Me (10 MCQs) | Generate ten practice multiple-choice questions. |
| ✍️ Solve Step-by-Step | Understand the working behind practice problems. |
| 📑 Assignment Guidance | Receive guidance on assignment structure and content. |

**Multilingual Learning Support**

Students have different language preferences and learning needs.

DueSoon supports ten languages:

1. English
2. Urdu
3. Pashto
4. Arabic
5. Hindi
6. Persian
7. Spanish
8. French
9. German
10. Chinese

English is the default language, while students can select their preferred language.

Right-to-left language rendering is supported for Urdu, Arabic, and Pashto.

This makes learning assistance more accessible to students who understand complex concepts more comfortably in their native languages.

### 3.6 Subject-Wise Task and Assignment Management

Every subject has an individual task management section.

Students can create tasks with the following information:

**Task Categories:**
- Assignment
- Quiz
- Exam
- Reading
- General Task

**Priority Levels:**
- High
- Medium
- Low

**Other details:**
- Task title.
- Deadline.
- Completion status.

Tasks are organized into Pending and Completed sections.

Students can track their progress and keep their academic responsibilities organized.

### 3.7 Master To-Do List

Managing tasks individually across multiple subjects can become difficult.

The Master To-Do List solves this problem by combining academic tasks from all subjects into one centralized task board.

**Key features:**
- View tasks from all enrolled subjects.
- Filter tasks by subject.
- View all, pending, or completed tasks.
- Search for specific tasks.
- Track assignments, quizzes, examinations, and study milestones.
- Update task completion status.

Students can understand their complete academic workload from one place.

### 3.8 Intelligent Study Planner

DueSoon includes a workload-aware study planner that helps students allocate their available study hours according to upcoming deadlines.

**Key features:**
- Set daily study-hour targets.
- Customize study availability for individual dates.
- Generate study schedules automatically.
- Combine manually pinned study sessions with automatic scheduling.
- Prioritize tasks using an earliest-deadline-first scheduling approach.
- Identify tasks that may not fit before their deadlines.
- Track missed study sessions.

The planner helps students distribute their workload instead of leaving everything until the examination period.

### 3.9 Grade Tracking and Academic Progress

Students can track their academic performance and understand what they need to achieve in their remaining assessments.

**Key features:**
- Track assignment and examination marks.
- Monitor graded coursework weight.
- Calculate current academic averages.
- View earned points.
- Calculate the average required in remaining coursework to reach a target grade.

This helps students make more informed decisions about their academic preparation.

Academic development is not limited to university coursework.

Students also need access to internships, research programs, fellowships, and professional development opportunities.

DueSoon provides a centralized, searchable opportunity catalog.

**Opportunity Categories:**

**Fields of Interest:**

- Software Engineering

- Robotics

**Work Modes:**

**Opportunity information includes:**

## 8. Installation and Setup

### Requirements

- Python 3.10 or later.
- Git.
- A modern web browser.
- An AI API key if AI assistant functionality is required.

### Step 1: Clone the Repository

```bash
git clone https://github.com/YOUR-USERNAME/DueSoon-MyB-BIC.git
cd DueSoon-MyB-BIC
```

### Step 2: Create a Virtual Environment

```bash
python -m venv .venv
```

Activate it on Windows PowerShell:

```powershell
.venv\Scripts\Activate.ps1
```

On Linux, macOS, or Git Bash:

```bash
source .venv/bin/activate
```

### Step 3: Install Dependencies

```bash
pip install -r requirements.txt
```

### Step 4: Configure AI Assistance 

Create a `.env` file in the project root and configure the provider you want to use.

Example:

```env
AI_PROVIDER=gemini
GEMINI_API_KEY=your_api_key_here
```

Alternatively, configure the supported provider and corresponding environment variables implemented in your application.

**Important:** Use only a provider and model configuration supported by your current application code. Never commit real API keys, passwords, or other secrets to GitHub.

### Step 5: Run the Application

```bash
python run.py
```

Open the following address in your browser:

```text
http://127.0.0.1:5000
```

The DueSoon application should now be available locally.
